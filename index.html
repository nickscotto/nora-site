Meet Nora

Nora has spent her career behind the chair and running her own salons on Maryland's Eastern Shore. She founded Europa Salon in Cambridge, was its lead stylist, and grew it into a two-time award winner with clients who followed her for years.

After selling Europa, she opened Nourish & Style in Easton, a smaller studio where she could focus on what she loves most: color, done carefully, one client at a time. That's exactly how she works now in Melbourne.

A hair appointment is more than a style. It's a conversation. Nora welcomes every client like family, and wants you to leave feeling confident, connected and cared for.

Good to know

Where do I go for my appointment?

Nora works as an independent specialist inside Euphoria Day Spa & Salon, 7640 N Wickham Rd, Suite 111, in Melbourne. Park out front, check in at the front desk, and ask for Nora.

How do you decide what color I get?

At your consultation. Nora looks at your hair, talks through what you want, and recommends what will work for your hair.

Should I bring photos of what I want?

Not required. If you have a few you love, text them to Nora at 443-521-0660 before your visit, but showing up with nothing in mind works just as well.

How often should I come in for color?

Most clients come back every 6 to 8 weeks for a refresh. Nora will map out timing for your hair specifically.

What if I need to reschedule?

Use the link in your confirmation text to move your appointment. Please give at least 24 hours' notice so the time can go to someone on the waitlist.

What if I don't love the color?

If it isn't what you discussed at your consult, Nora will fix it that same day.

Why do you ask for a card when I book?

It holds your appointment. Nothing is charged until your visit, unless you miss it without notice.

What's the before & after photo about?

Nora takes a quick photo before and after your color so you have a real record of the change. It's texted to you to keep. Whether it's ever used anywhere else is up to you, and she'll always ask first.
